 2026-07-27
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  + Metrics per project and per day: sessions, prompts, events, tool calls,
    errors, subagents, tokens and size on disk, with a bar chart and rankings
    of tools, models and subagent types. The server walks the transcripts once
    (0.7 s for 123) and caches per file: the second time is 6 ms.      [pend.]
  + Neighbourhood: every live session at once, each with a plan of its
    stations (they light up when used and carry their count), what it is doing,
    its subagents and its context. One click enters that room. Background
    sessions show their job's last report, and the station is only highlighted
    while the session is working: waiting for you is not being anywhere.
                                                                       [194a6c9]
  ! The inspector showed up at the top, out of its box, and the history had no
    padding or scroll: a duplicated selector invalidated every rule after it.
                                                                      [b0c0bd1]
  ! With the Camp banner (or the legend) open, tooltips of whatever was
    underneath still fired: Pixi listens for the mouse on the document and
    does not know there is a panel in front.                          [540f4fc]
  + Background jobs are visible at last: the Camp, in the bottom corner. Its
    sign counts them and clicking it unfolds a banner with all of them: state,
    project, last report and one click to open its conversation. A job
    claiming to work with no process behind it shows up as stale.
                                                             [e696f79] [f1d87fd]
  + Keyboard shortcuts in the player: space (or K) plays and pauses, arrows
    move one event, with shift ten, Home and End jump to the ends and
    up/down change the speed.                                          [d6890fe]
  * Continuous integration on GitHub Actions: types, tests against a seeded
    ~/.claude, and the world mounted in a real Chromium.               [d6890fe]
  * @fastify/static up to date (4 path traversal advisories closed).   [d6890fe]
  ! The history index could point at an already deleted transcript and the
    request died with ENOENT; the cache was also shared between different
    worlds.                                                            [d6890fe]
  ! "npm run demo -- --keep" did not give the terminal back.           [d6890fe]
  + Light theme next to the dark one, with a single button beside the
    languages. The stage changes too: the scene has its own palette,
    because a canvas does not inherit CSS variables.                   [b9bcd06]
  + History can be browsed as a project tree, with collapsible sessions,
    a count and a size per project.                                    [b9bcd06]
  + Timeline width is resizable by dragging the splitter; double-click
    restores the default. Remembered across sessions.                 [749d69c]
  + Dismissing the retention notice leaves a permanent one-liner, with a
    "why?" that brings it back.                                       [749d69c]
  * Clicking a session in the history jumps to the world right away,
    without waiting for the transcript to load.                        [b9bcd06]
  * The player now reaches the end of long conversations: history is
    fetched in 1000-event chunks as it advances.                      [f95783a]
  + The history warns about what Claude Code has already deleted (30 days
    by default) and explains how to keep more.                        [f95783a]
  ! The skip-to-end button stopped at 1024 of 1586.                   [f95783a]
